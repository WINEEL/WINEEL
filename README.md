- **Data Center Networking Research (CSEN 329):** Survey-style academic research project analyzing modern data center networking. Studied three-tier, spine-leaf, Clos, and fat-tree architectures, core protocols such as VLANs, ECMP, VXLAN, and SDN, along with security, resiliency, and AI usage in data center operations.  
  - Repo: https://github.com/WINEEL/data-center-networks-research

- **SmartRecs:** Hybrid movie recommender using IMDb datasets with TF-IDF content similarity and SVD-based collaborative filtering with genre awareness.

- **QuickBill** and **MyCalc** in C. QuickBill handles CSV-based product management and billing. MyCalc behaves like a Python-style REPL with BODMAS, exponentiation, and nested parentheses.

- **GardenSim:** JavaFX-based garden simulator applying OOADP concepts such as seasons, weather, pests, sensors, and survivability tests.
